# 2026-10-03

## Session moved to Obsidian's secret storage

- The session (cookies) left `data.json` for `app.secretStorage` (per vault, per device), under `sessionSecretName` (default `knowii-community-session`). Reason: `data.json` travels with the vault (git, Syncthing, cloud). Decision: Sébastien.
- No one signs in again: the legacy `session` field stays as a read-only bootstrap; every device copies it into its own secret storage on load (and on `onExternalSettingsChange`). Removed 60 days after the first migration (`legacySecretMigratedAt`), by the "Remove plain-text copy now" button, on a new sign-in, sign-out or Forget. Refreshed cookies go to the device's secret only; other devices keep their own copy.
- New module `src/app/domain/session-secret.ts`; specs `session-secret.spec.ts`, `plugin-session-secret.spec.ts` (device B bootstrap, refresh, rotate, sign-out, 60-day purge, button purge).
- `minAppVersion` already 1.13.0 (secret storage needs 1.11.4).

## Opt-in sharing (after 1.5.1)

- 1.5.1 left phones without a way to get a session (they cannot sign in). Decision (Sébastien): opt-in "Share session with my other devices" (`shareSessionAcrossDevices`). On: `data.json` keeps an up-to-date plain-text copy (as before 1.5.1), it wins over the device's secret, never purged. Default on for members who had a session in `data.json` at migration (or a migration date), off for fresh installs; decided once, persisted. Turning off deletes the copy. Sign-out clears both. Mobile card points to the setting.
