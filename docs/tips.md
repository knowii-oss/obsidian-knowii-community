# Tips

- **Dock it**: set "Open the pane in" to a sidebar and lower the zoom to keep the community one glance away while you write.
- **Hotkeys**: bind **Open Knowii: messages** and **Open Knowii: notifications** to check in without touching the mouse.
- **Share a note**: copy the text you want to discuss, jump to the right space with the toolbar, and paste it into a new post.
- **Catch up fast**: right-click the Knowii ribbon icon to see the latest unread items and open one in a click.
- **Only the unread**: type `is:unread` in the "what's new" list, add words to narrow it down (`is:unread obsidian`).
- **Keep it tidy**: Ctrl+J/K to move, Ctrl/Cmd+Enter to mark as read, Ctrl+E to archive, Ctrl/Cmd+S to save as a note, Ctrl/Cmd+R to reply; "Archive read" clears everything you have read.
- **Build your knowledge**: save the answers you get in the community as notes, then link them from your own notes.
- **Too chatty?** Switch off the kinds you don't care about (likes, new members…) and mute busy spaces under **Watch the whole community**.
- **Something looks off?** Reload the pane, or open the page in your browser from the toolbar.
